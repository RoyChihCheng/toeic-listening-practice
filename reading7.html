Part 5: Incomplete Sentences (單句填空)

Directions: A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given below each sentence. Select the best answer to complete the sentence. (Questions 101–130)

101.
Warehouse staff must label every shipment ______ so that packages are not sent to the wrong address.
Chap 1: 詞性判斷（副詞修飾動詞）
題目：Warehouse staff must label every shipment ______ so that packages are not sent to the wrong address.

中譯：倉庫人員必須正確標示每件貨物，以免包裹寄錯地址。

【文法詳解】：空格修飾動詞 label（標示得如何），需要副詞 accurately。故選 (D)。

【選項解析】：(A) accurate 為形容詞；(B) accuracy 與 (C) accurateness 為名詞，皆不能修飾動詞。

【核心單字與搭配詞】：label (v.) 標示；shipment (n.) 貨件；accurately (adv.) 正確地
102.
The store manager decided to handle the customer's complaint ______ rather than refer it to head office.
代名詞（反身代名詞作強調用法）
題目：The store manager decided to handle the customer's complaint ______ rather than refer it to head office.

中譯：店經理決定親自處理這位顧客的投訴，而不是轉交總公司。

【文法詳解】：句子結構在 complaint 之後已經完整，空格用來強調「由店經理本人」處理，需用反身代名詞 herself 作強調用法，指涉主詞 The store manager。故選 (D)。

【選項解析】：(B) her 為受格，handle 已有受詞 the complaint；(A) she 為主格，句中已有主詞；(C) hers 為所有格代名詞，語意不通。

【核心單字與搭配詞】：handle (v.) 處理；complaint (n.) 投訴；refer … to (phr.) 將……轉交給；head office (n.) 總公司
103.
All fire extinguishers in the building are ______ by a licensed contractor twice each year.
Chap 3: 主動與被動語態（be + p.p. 被動語態）
題目：All fire extinguishers in the building are ______ by a licensed contractor twice each year.

中譯：大樓內所有滅火器每年由持照承包商檢查兩次。

【文法詳解】：主詞 fire extinguishers 是「被檢查」的對象，且空格後有 by a licensed contractor，須用被動語態 are inspected。故選 (D)。

【選項解析】：(A) inspecting 為主動進行式；(B) inspection 為名詞；(C) inspects 為主動現在式，與主詞及 by 片語皆不合。

【核心單字與搭配詞】：fire extinguisher (n.) 滅火器；licensed (adj.) 持有執照的；contractor (n.) 承包商
104.
______ the store was crowded on the first day of the sale, most customers were served within ten minutes.
Chap 4: 副詞子句與連接詞（表讓步的從屬連接詞）
題目：______ the store was crowded on the first day of the sale, most customers were served within ten minutes.

中譯：雖然特賣第一天店裡很擁擠，大多數顧客仍在十分鐘內獲得服務。

【文法詳解】：空格後為完整子句 the store was crowded，需要連接詞；前後語意為讓步關係，故選 (C) Although。

【選項解析】：(A) In spite of 與 (D) Due to 為介系詞片語，不能接子句；(B) Nevertheless 為副詞，不能引導子句。

【核心單字與搭配詞】：crowded (adj.) 擁擠的；serve (v.) 服務、接待
105.
The accounting department announced that a complete ______ of all travel expenses will be carried out next month.
Chap 1: 詞性判斷（名詞作主詞）
題目：The accounting department announced that a complete ______ of all travel expenses will be carried out next month.

中譯：會計部門宣布下個月將對所有差旅費用進行全面稽核。

【文法詳解】：that 子句的主詞為 a complete ______，空格位於形容詞 complete 之後、of 之前，需要可數名詞 audit（稽核）。故選 (C)。

【選項解析】：(B) auditing 為動名詞，前方不加 a complete；(A) audited 為過去分詞；(D) auditor 為「稽核員」，語意不合。

【核心單字與搭配詞】：audit (n.) 稽核；travel expenses (n.) 差旅費；carry out (phr.) 執行
106.
The hotel's new booking system allows guests to ______ their reservations online up to 24 hours before arrival.
Business Vocabulary: 商務核心動詞辨析（modify a reservation 修改訂房）
題目：The hotel's new booking system allows guests to ______ their reservations online up to 24 hours before arrival.

中譯：飯店的新訂房系統讓房客最晚可在抵達前 24 小時線上修改訂房內容。

【文法詳解】：本題考動詞語意。受詞為 their reservations（訂房），能與之搭配的動詞為 (D) modify（修改）；modify/change/cancel a reservation 為飯店業常見用語。

【選項解析】：(A) mobilize 意為「動員」；(B) motivate 意為「激勵」；(C) multiply 意為「增加、相乘」，皆不能與 reservations 搭配。

【核心單字與搭配詞】：booking system (n.) 訂房系統；modify (v.) 修改；reservation (n.) 預訂；in advance (phr.) 事先
107.
The committee chose the supplier ______ samples performed best in the durability test.
Chap 6: 關係子句（所有格關係代名詞 whose）
題目：The committee chose the supplier ______ samples performed best in the durability test.

中譯：委員會選擇了樣品在耐久性測試中表現最好的那家供應商。

【文法詳解】：空格後接名詞 samples，表示「供應商的樣品」，需用所有格關係代名詞 whose。故選 (B)。

【選項解析】：(A) who 與 (D) whom 後方不能直接接名詞；(C) which 後接名詞會造成子句缺主詞。

【核心單字與搭配詞】：supplier (n.) 供應商；durability (n.) 耐久性
108.
The installation of new carpeting on the third and fourth floors ______ scheduled to finish before Friday.
Chap 2: 動詞型態與主詞動詞一致性（主詞為單數名詞片語）
題目：The installation of new carpeting on the third and fourth floors ______ scheduled to finish before Friday.

中譯：三樓與四樓新地毯的鋪設預計在週五前完工。

【文法詳解】：主詞為單數名詞 The installation，後方的 of new carpeting on the third and fourth floors 為修飾語，動詞須用單數 is。故選 (A)。

【選項解析】：(C) are、(B) were、(D) have been 皆為複數形，與單數主詞不一致。

【核心單字與搭配詞】：installation (n.) 安裝、鋪設；carpeting (n.) 地毯
109.
The quarterly sales meeting will begin ______ 9:30 A.M. sharp in the main conference room.
商務介系詞（時間介系詞 at + 時刻）
題目：The quarterly sales meeting will begin ______ 9:30 A.M. sharp in the main conference room.

中譯：季度業務會議將於上午九點三十分準時在大會議室開始。

【文法詳解】：表示「在某個確切時刻」時，英文使用介系詞 at（at 9:30 A.M. / at noon）。故選 (A)。

【選項解析】：(C) on 用於日期與星期（on Monday / on May 3）；(D) in 用於月份、年份或較長時段（in March / in the morning）；(B) by 表「最晚在……之前」，與 begin … sharp（準時開始）語意不合。

【核心單字與搭配詞】：quarterly (adj.) 每季的；sharp (adv.) 準時地；conference room (n.) 會議室
110.
The manager confirmed that ______ the morning shift nor the evening shift had reported any problems with the new registers.
Chap 8: 比較級與相關連接詞（neither … nor 配對）

中譯：經理確認早班和晚班都沒有回報新收銀機有任何問題。

【文法詳解】：空格後方出現 nor，須與 neither 配對。故選 (B)。

【選項解析】：(C) either 搭配 or；(D) both 搭配 and；(A) whether 搭配 or，皆不與 nor 配對。

【核心單字與搭配詞】：shift (n.) 輪班；register (n.) 收銀機
111.
Refunds will not be issued ______ the customer presents the original receipt.
Chap 4: 副詞子句與連接詞（unless 除非）
題目：Refunds will not be issued ______ the customer presents the original receipt.

中譯：除非顧客出示原始收據，否則不予退款。

【文法詳解】：空格後為完整子句 the customer presents…，需要連接詞；語意為「除非」，故選 (B) unless。

【選項解析】：(D) without、(C) except、(A) in case of 皆為介系詞（片語），不能接子句。

112.
The seminar registration fee covers lunch ______ all printed course materials.
Chap 8: 比較級與相關連接詞（連接兩個名詞的 as well as）
題目：The seminar registration fee covers lunch ______ all printed course materials.

中譯：研討會報名費包含午餐以及所有印刷的課程教材。

【文法詳解】：空格前後分別為名詞 lunch 與名詞片語 all printed course materials，需要能連接兩個名詞的片語連接詞；as well as（以及）可直接連接兩個名詞。故選 (C)。

【選項解析】：(D) in addition 與 (B) moreover 為連接副詞，需以逗號隔開並連接句子，不能直接連接兩個名詞；(A) also 為副詞，通常放在動詞前，不能作連接詞使用。

【核心單字與搭配詞】：registration fee (n.) 報名費；cover (v.) 包含、涵蓋；course materials (n.) 課程教材

120.
The next-generation magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scanner is ______ faster than older diagnostic systems, reducing patient scan times by half.

題目：The next-generation magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scanner is ______ faster than older diagnostic systems, reducing patient scan times by half.

125.
The new delivery van is not as ______ as the older model, but it can carry twice as much cargo.
Chap 8: 比較級與最高級（as + 原級 + as 的同等比較）
題目：The new delivery van is not as ______ as the older model, but it can carry twice as much cargo.

中譯：新的送貨廂型車雖然不如舊款快，但能載運兩倍的貨物。
